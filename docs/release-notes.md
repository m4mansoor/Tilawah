# Tilawah v0.1.0

**One Quran. Two ways to serve it.** 🕌

Tilawah is an open-source, non-profit app that **listens to your Quran recitation and corrects your mistakes** — word-by-word and tajweed-by-tajweed, powered by AI.

Whether you're a **Qari lending your voice** or a **Learner perfecting your recitation**, Tilawah gives instant, gentle feedback on every verse you recite.

---

## 📥 Download & install

| Platform | File | How to install |
|---|---|---|
| **Windows** | `Tilawah_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe` | Run the installer. If SmartScreen appears, click **More info → Run anyway**. |
| **Windows (alt)** | `Tilawah_0.1.0_x64_en-US.msi` | Same as above. |
| **macOS — Apple Silicon** | `Tilawah_0.1.0_aarch64.dmg` | Open the `.dmg` and drag **Tilawah** into **Applications**. First launch: right-click the app → **Open**. |
| **macOS — Intel** | `Tilawah_0.1.0_x64.dmg` | Same as above. |
| **Android** | `app-universal-release.apk` | Download on your phone, allow **install from unknown sources** when prompted, then tap the file to install. |

> ⚠️ **Note:** The desktop apps (Windows / macOS) are currently **unsigned**, so your OS shows a security warning on first launch — that's expected and safe to accept. The Android app is signed.

## 🎙️ For Qaris — Lend your voice

Your recitation becomes a **waqf of sound** — a lasting gift the whole ummah learns from.

1. Tap **🎙️ Record** and allow microphone access.
2. Recite a verse **slowly and clearly** (a great place to start is **Al-Fatihah**).
3. Tap **⏹ Stop & Check**.
4. Review your **transcript** and the **word-by-word comparison** — every mistake is highlighted next to the correct pronunciation.
5. Study the **tajweed rules** shown for that verse.
6. Keep your cleanest take — and submit your recitations to the collection at **https://tilawah.me**.

---

## 📖 For Learners — Learn to recite

Get precise, gentle feedback on every single word.

1. Tap **🎙️ Record** and recite any verse you're learning.
2. Tilawah shows you:
   - your **transcript**,
   - the **matched verse**,
   - a **word-by-word correction** (what you said → what's correct),
   - the **tajweed rules** in that verse.
3. Read the corrections carefully, then tap **🎙️ Recite Again** and try once more.
4. Repeat until you recite it correctly — **Masha'Allah**.
