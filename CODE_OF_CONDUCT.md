# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Open Quran Engine is a community *waqf* — a gift for the whole ummah. We are
committed to making participation a welcoming, respectful experience for
everyone, regardless of background, skill level, or opinion.

## Our Standards

- Be kind, patient, and respectful.
- Assume good faith; give constructive feedback.
- No harassment, hate speech, or discrimination.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers. Maintainers will review the
matter and take appropriate action (warning, removal, or ban).
